How It Works

1️⃣

Report

Take a photo of the civic issue, describe it, and submit with your location.

2️⃣

AI Analyzes

The system classifies the issue, determines priority, and routes to the right department.

3️⃣

Officer Acts

Municipal officers receive the complaint and work on resolving the issue.

4️⃣

Resolved

Once fixed, officers upload proof and you get notified. Issue closed!
